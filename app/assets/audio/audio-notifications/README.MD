# Audio Notification Assets

A curated collection of 20 high-quality, lightweight, synthetically engineered `.wav` notification alerts designed specifically for health, fitness, habit-tracking, and workout applications. 

All sounds in this repository are **100% Royalty-Free** and open-source. They can be used freely in personal, educational, and commercial production environments without attribution restrictions.

## 📁 Repository Structure & Asset Manifest

The project is divided cleanly into two logical asset directories representing functional status conditions.

### 🟢 `/positive`
*High-energy, encouraging, and motivational audio alerts used to signal achievements, zone maintenance, milestones, and start states.*

1. **`boxingBell_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* High-pitched, resonant metallic ping mimicking a classic ring announcer bell.
   * *Best For:* Boxing rounds, HIIT station rotations, or circuit start signals.
2. **`cyberCountdown_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Futuristic, rhythmic 3-step electronic step scale finishing on a high note.
   * *Best For:* Automated prep intervals, set count-ins, or "3-2-1" sprint starts.
3. **`zenGong_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Deep, rich, low-frequency acoustic chord with long-tail harmonic resonance.
   * *Best For:* Yoga posture hold completions, guided meditation intervals, or final cool downs.
4. **`cardioPulse_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Organic, fast-paced dual electronic bleeps mimicking an optimal pulse rate.
   * *Best For:* Heart-rate target zone locking or quick step-tempo synchronization.
5. **`heavyIronLift_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Deep, low-end industrial sawtooth clang with a heavy compression drop.
   * *Best For:* Heavy lifting milestones, barbell set completions, or strength-training logs.
6. **`theSprinter_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Crisp, piercing square-wave burst modeled after an electronic referee whistle.
   * *Best For:* Speed intervals, linear sprint triggers, or track drills.
7. **`retroArcadeJump_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Playful, highly energetic rising 8-bit digital synth sweep.
   * *Best For:* Gamified exercise progression, XP gains, or closing daily workout metrics.
8. **`deepBreathIn_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Soft, ambient, ethereal sine-wave swell that mimics standard respiratory patterns.
   * *Best For:* Stretching transitions, Pilates sets, or mindful breath work.
9. **`theDailyStreak_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* An elegant, sparkling major-7th melodic arpeggio.
   * *Best For:* Calendar multi-day milestones, streak tracking notifications, or consistency trophies.
10. **`laserTarget_alert.wav`**
    * *Acoustic Profile:* Sharp, directional high-to-low dynamic audio sweep.
    * *Best For:* Precision movement tracking, form targets hit, or custom workout milestones.

### 🔴 `/negative`
*Dissonant, lower-pitch, or dampening audio alerts used to indicate rest periods, form failures, pace drops, or streak interruptions.*

1. **`negative_flatline_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Sudden, blunt frequency drop falling exactly one full octave.
   * *Best For:* Device disconnections, skipped intervals, or target failures.
2. **`negative_heavySigh_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Low-frequency, slowly deflating linear triangle-wave sweep.
   * *Best For:* Form correction warnings or pacing reduction indicators.
3. **`negative_retroGameOver_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Downward-stepping glitchy 8-bit sawtooth sequence.
   * *Best For:* Complete session timeouts, routine failure thresholds, or cancelled timers.
4. **`negative_buzzerError_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* High-friction, clashing dual-square wave industrial buzz.
   * *Best For:* Illegal movement patterns, rep disqualifications, or critical boundaries breached.
5. **`negative_zoneDrop_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Melancholic, falling dual-tone sine-wave chord.
   * *Best For:* Falling out of target fat-burn or optimal cardio heart-rate zones.
6. **`negative_unbalanced_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Drastic, unstable pitch modulation mimicking physical shakiness.
   * *Best For:* Posture misalignment, center-of-mass warnings, or uneven weight distribution alerts.
7. **`negative_staticFriction_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Aggressive, grinding high-frequency micro white noise burst.
   * *Best For:* Sudden premature workout pauses, tracking errors, or forced emergency stops.
8. **`negative_theSlide_alert.wav`**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Fast, cartoon-inspired exponential descending frequency sweep.

9. **`negative_streakBroken_alert.wav`**

10. **`negative_timeOut_alert.wav`**
